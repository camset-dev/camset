# 1. Record decisions as ADRs

- **Date:** 2026-11-03
- **Status:** accepted
- **Rule:** Each decision is one dated record here, in context, decision and consequences; a changed decision gets a new
  record that supersedes the old one.

## Context

The owner's rules and the project's choices were written in three places that had drifted apart: the kit plan's standing rules (stale: they still forbade Auto Power OFF Temp.), the playbook and the agents' notes.

## Decision

Each decision is one Architecture Decision Record here (Michael Nygard's form: context, decision, consequences), dated, never rewritten. A changed decision gets a new record that supersedes the old one.

## Consequences

Other documents link to a record instead of restating it. The playbook keeps its own rules: they are the defaults for someone else's camera, not this owner's decisions.

# 8. By hand only: USB, language, region and network identity

- **Date:** 2026-10 (first kit); Auto Power OFF Temp. allowed 2026-11-02
- **Status:** accepted

## Context

These settings can take the camera off the link or change what every other setting means (NTSC/PAL re-indexes frame rates).

## Decision

USB settings, Language, NTSC/PAL, Area/Date and network identity are changed by the owner, with the closed watcher downloading after each. Auto Power OFF Temp. may be changed remotely (the owner: "it causes no issues when in stills").

## Consequences

Proofs of these rows come from the owner's hand changes.
